# Academics card photos

Save a photo here with one of these file names (`.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp`) and that
school's card on the home page uses it automatically. No code change is needed; just
restart `npm run dev` or run `npm run build` again.

| File name           | Card                             |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| `nursing`           | College of Nursing and Research  |
| `pharmacy`          | College of Pharmacy              |
| `allied-health`     | School of Allied Health Sciences |
| `physiotherapy`     | School of Physiotherapy          |

Cards without a photo keep the navy illustrated banner. The card shows the image at
400 × 344 (about 7:6), cropped to fill. The school name is drawn over the top-left
corner on a dark fade, so keep that corner free of faces and busy detail.

## AI image prompts

These match the style of the Engineering card photo. Add the shared style line to the
end of each prompt.

**Shared style:** photorealistic, candid, bright natural daylight, modern Indian
university campus, students aged 18 to 22, shallow depth of field, warm and optimistic
mood, 7:6 aspect ratio, calm and slightly darker top-left corner, no text, no logos, no
watermark.

- **nursing:** Indian nursing students in navy-blue scrubs practising patient care on a
  training mannequin in a modern hospital simulation lab, an instructor guiding them,
  stethoscopes, an IV stand and patient monitors in the background.
- **pharmacy:** Indian pharmacy students in white lab coats compounding and measuring
  medicines in a modern pharmaceutical lab, glass beakers, an analytical balance, pill
  bottles and shelves of labelled medicines.
- **allied-health:** Indian allied health science students in lab coats using
  microscopes and analysing samples in a clinical diagnostics laboratory, test tubes, a
  centrifuge and a medical imaging screen in the background.
- **physiotherapy:** Indian physiotherapy students treating a patient's knee on a
  treatment table in a bright rehabilitation clinic, exercise balls, resistance bands
  and parallel bars in the background.
